About Us

Investing in Collectibles. Building Value.

GW Capital Partners LLC was founded by Jeffrey T. Whitcomb Jr. and Joseph V. Galto III with a shared passion for collectibles, investing, and entrepreneurship.

We believe collectibles are more than hobbies. They are alternative assets with long-term value potential. Our approach combines market knowledge, disciplined buying, grading strategy, and professional resale practices.

To create long-term value through strategic collectible investments while maintaining integrity, transparency, and professionalism.
